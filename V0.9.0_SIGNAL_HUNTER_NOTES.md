# Signal Hunter — AP Baseline Watch (v0.9.0 development patch)

Implemented: Persistent saved AP baseline, on-demand scan comparison by BSSID, new AP session log with timestamp/SSID/RSSI/channel, persistent Trusted/Investigate/Ignore classifications, and Signal Hunter navigation.

Not yet implemented: Background monitoring and Android notifications, durable alert history, repeated-detection confirmation, signal trend graphs, and export. New AP does not prove a mobile hotspot or surveillance device. Android may throttle or cache scans. Source not compiled or device-tested.
