Screaming Weasels Wi-Fi Teaser v0.8.7 — DEVELOPMENT SOURCE

Based on the user-tested v0.8.6 source ZIP.

Changes:
- Export latest completed Ping and Speed diagnostics to CSV and PDF from the Speed tab.
- Ping report includes target, ICMP payload size, requested/actual duration, sent/received packets, loss, min/average/max RTT, and average successive RTT variation (jitter proxy).
- Speed report includes HTTPS download/upload Mbps and DNS HTTPS response timing. It explicitly marks ICMP average latency and jitter as not measured in the speed test.
- Mustard-yellow primary action buttons across Wi-Fi, Speed, AP naming and Device Scan; the Survey retains its green idle/mustard active behavior.
- Existing on-demand Device Scan deep investigation preserved, with hostname, selected TCP ports, response details and uncertainty notices.

Limitations:
- Device Scan does not infer MAC/vendor, OS, or UDP service state without supporting observations.
- This build does not claim live speed-test jitter measurements.
- Reports export the latest in-memory Ping and Speed results; they are not yet a multi-session archive.
- The Android app has not been compiled in this environment (no Android SDK/Gradle wrapper executable available). Build and test in Android Studio.

Build: Open OfficeWifiInspector in Android Studio and Build > Rebuild Project.
